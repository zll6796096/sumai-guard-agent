# 3-Minute Demo Script

## 1. Upload a Photo

Open `http://localhost:8081`.

Upload a 玄関 or 浴室 photo and select the matching room hint. Explain that the app asks for no elderly questionnaire because the POC works from one photo only.

## 2. Show Red Boxes

Click `AIで安全チェック`.

Show the annotated photo with red boxes and R labels. Explain that the red boxes are visible-risk communication markers, not measurement or construction output.

## 3. Show Basis

Open the リスク詳細 section.

Point out:

- Why the area may be dangerous.
- The photo-based evidence.
- The cautious demo basis label.
- Confidence and human-confirmation flags.

## 4. Show Three Action Cards

Review:

- `家族で今日できること`: no-cost actions only.
- `ケアマネ・福祉用具に相談`: purchase/rental/welfare-equipment consultation.
- `専門施工・現地確認`: professional construction or on-site confirmation.

Explain that deterministic policy routes actions after the AI vision result.

## 5. Show Re-Check Loop

Click `対策後の写真でもう一度チェック`.

Explain the intended loop: take a photo, act on safe next steps, take another photo, and compare whether visible risks were reduced.
